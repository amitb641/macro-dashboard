Methodology, Sources, and Validation

A disclosure of how the indicators are sourced, computed, validated, and presented — and where artificial intelligence is used in the production process.
PUBLISHED weekly · Fri 06:00 UTC METHODOLOGY VERSION 2026.05 LAST REVIEWED 2026-05-13

ABSTRACT     The dashboard reports on twelve U.S. macroeconomic and financial-sector indicators sourced from five public data providers, supplemented by quarterly earnings disclosures from five major U.S. banks. Indicator values are computed by deterministic code from the published source series; written commentary accompanying each indicator is drafted by a large-language model under documented constraints (§05) and reviewed against a ten-control validation framework (§04) before publication.

§ 01

Scope

what is reported

The dashboard tracks twelve indicators across four domains: growth (GDP, payrolls), labour (U-3 and U-6 unemployment, wages, jobless claims), prices (CPI, core CPI, PCE), credit and rates (Treasury yields, IG/HY spreads, mortgage rates, card delinquency), and a fifth view on the U.S. banking sector sourced from the largest five depository institutions' quarterly disclosures (JPM, BAC, WFC, GS, C). Each indicator's definition, formula, threshold rules, and confirmation logic is documented in METHODOLOGY.md.

§ 03

Indicator Computation

deterministic · METHODOLOGY.md

Indicator values are computed by deterministic Python from the source series. No AI is used in the computation of any published number. Representative formulas:

Thresholds for indicator commentary (e.g. payrolls below 150K denotes deceleration, U-3 above cycle low denotes labour weakening, etc.) are codified in data/playbook.md §1 and applied by the analyzer step.

§ 04

Validation Controls

build-blocking · 10 controls

Every published edition passes through ten controls. Any critical-severity divergence suspends publication; the briefing email is not sent until the divergence is resolved or downgraded by the editor.

ControlVerifiesSeverity
C-01Internal consistency: every value shown on the page reconciles to the underlying JSONcritical
C-02Source verification: every published value traces to a raw observation from a §02 sourcecritical
C-03Freshness: each series is no older than its known publish-lag baselinewarning
C-04Verbatim verification: every quoted bank commentary span exists verbatim in the archived transcriptcritical
C-05Schema conformance: signals.json and raw_data.json match the contractcritical
C-06Seed drift: deterministic outputs unchanged between identical inputswarning
C-07Collector errors: any source-fetch failure recorded for reviewwarning
C-08Cross-source agreement: shared anchor metrics reconcile between FRED and BLScritical
C-09Visual quality: 224 layout, typography, color, and spacing checks against style_guide.mdwarning
C-10Independent visual review by a multimodal AI; flags rendered-chart issues that the DOM-based checks cannot seewarning

Verdicts from all ten controls are aggregated into a single go/no-go decision (PASS / WARN / FAIL / SKIP). The publish step reads only this aggregated verdict and refuses to send on FAIL.

§ 05

Use of Artificial Intelligence

disclosure
Where AI is used

Four of the eleven production steps use an Anthropic Claude language model. None of them compute or alter any published numeric value; their outputs are limited to written commentary, methodology-based diagnostics, and quality review.

StepRole of AIBounded byFailure handling
AIPer-tab commentaryDrafts 2–4 sentences accompanying each indicator panel. Sentence-count and vocabulary rules are enforced after generation.Copy rules (no hedging, no fabricated numerics, ≤4 sentences); verified against C-01.Sentence rewritten or removed
AIEarnings extractionExtracts pre-tagged fields (economy, lending, credit, outlook) from archived bank transcripts.Verbatim verification (C-04) against archived transcript text.Edition blocks publication if any quote diverges
AIVisual reviewIndependent review of rendered charts against the visual contract; flags issues invisible to DOM checks.Read-only; output is a flag, never an edit.Editor reviews; warning only
AIDiagnostic commentaryReads validation findings and recurrence history; classifies each finding (first-time vs. recurring) for the editor's review queue.Read-only observer; cannot modify code, data, or layout.Findings logged; no action taken

All AI use is subject to four operational controls:

Circuit-breaker

A single configuration flag stops all AI calls system-wide. Used during incidents or model deprecations.

env: AGENT_DISABLE_ALL
Cost ceiling

Per-edition call count is capped; production exceeds neither the budget nor the model rate-limit.

env: AGENT_MAX_LLM_CALLS
Path restriction

Each AI-using step may only write to a specifically permitted file path. The page layout and indicator computation paths are excluded.

scripts/_agent_guardrails.py
Audit trail

Every AI call records its prompt hash, model, token count, cost, and output destination to a rolling append-only log (2,000-entry FIFO).

data/agent_memory.jsonl
