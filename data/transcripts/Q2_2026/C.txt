SUMMARY Citigroup Inc. ( C +1.16% ) reported second-quarter net income of $5.8 billion, reflecting growth across its five core business segments and the achievement of its highest quarterly revenue in 10 years. Management stated that the firm generated positive operating leverage of over 9% as revenue growth outperformed expense increases. The company confirmed its full-year 2026 return on average tangible common equity target of 10% to 11% despite typical second-half seasonality in the markets business. Strategic focus remains on the organizational transformation, including the migration of consent order work to regulatory review and the acceleration of investments in wealth management and card acquisitions. CEO Fraser stated, "Nearly 9 out of 10 of our people are using our AI tools," noting that technology adoption is driving productivity and faster product launches such as Payment Express. Management reported that referrals from retail branches to Citigold increased 23%, which CEO Fraser described as a tangible benefit of integrating retail branches into the Wealth segment. CFO Lucchetti indicated that if market conditions remain constructive, the company may "lean in" by accelerating structural efficiency actions and potentially incurring higher severance in the second half of the year. The company expects to deconsolidate its ownership in the Mexico consumer business in early 2027, followed by an initial public offering when market conditions allow. Jennifer Landis will transition from Head of Investor Relations to Chief Financial Officer for Markets, with Margot Pillich assuming the role of Head of Strategy, M&A, and Investor Relations. The American Airlines co-branded card portfolio acquisition in April added over $6 billion in loans and more than 2 million accounts to the U.S. Consumer Cards segment. Management noted that institutional market share in the Services segment increased 120 basis points year over year, while client wins in that segment rose 36% during the same period. INDUSTRY GLOSSARY ROTCE : Return on average tangible common equity, a measure of how much profit a bank generates with the money shareholders have invested. CET1 : Common Equity Tier 1 capital ratio, a key measure of a bank's financial strength and ability to absorb losses. NNIA : Net new investment assets, representing the net flow of client money into investment products. AUC/AUA : Assets under custody and/or administration, representing the total value of assets a financial institution manages or holds for clients. TTS : Treasury and Trade Solutions, a business unit providing cash management and trade finance services to corporations. G-SIB : Global Systemically Important Bank, a bank whose systemic risk is so great that its failure would trigger a global financial crisis. DTA : Deferred tax asset, an item on a balance sheet that reduces the amount of tax a company will owe in the future. ACL : Allowance for credit losses, a reserve established for estimated credit losses on loans. DFAST : Dodd-Frank Act Stress Test, a forward-looking exercise conducted by the Federal Reserve to help ensure financial institutions can lend to households and businesses during a recession. SEV : Likely referring to the Stress Capital Buffer (SCB), an additional capital requirement for banks based on their performance in annual stress tests.
