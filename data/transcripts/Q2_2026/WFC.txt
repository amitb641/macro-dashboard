SUMMARY Wells Fargo & Company ( WFC +1.22% ) reported diluted earnings per share of $2.00, an increase of 25% from the prior year. Management noted that results reflected broad-based revenue growth across all operating segments and the continued execution of efficiency initiatives, marked by the 24th consecutive quarter of headcount reductions. Following the removal of its asset cap, the company has increased its balance sheet through significant growth in commercial and consumer loans and an expansion of its markets business. Management indicated that capital deployment remains focused on areas such as credit cards, investment banking, and advisor hiring to drive long-term returns. The company maintained its full-year 2026 net interest income guidance while expressing confidence in achieving its medium-term return on average tangible common equity target of 17% to 18% if favorable economic conditions persist. Scharf noted that credit card vintages from 2022 to 2024 are now contributing to profitability, while larger 2025 and 2026 vintages currently offset some gains due to upfront marketing and onboarding costs. Management reported that mobile active users increased to 33.7 million, a gain of 1.6 million users from the prior year, supported by an improved digital customer experience. The company launched Advisor Gateway, a new technology platform featuring GenAI capabilities, intended to improve productivity and retention for financial advisors. Scharf indicated that the company is taking market share in investment banking, ranking number three in leveraged finance and number four in U.S. M&A advisory by announced deal volume year to date. Santomassimo stated that risk-weighted assets are expected to decrease by approximately 7% once new capital rules are finalized, based on current proposals. Management attributed the 31% increase in securities-based lending balances to the success of financial advisors offering these products to affluent clients. Scharf confirmed that while the markets business has grown its balance sheet by $200 billion since late 2024, management can adjust this activity if returns do not meet expectations. INDUSTRY GLOSSARY Asset Cap : A regulatory limit imposed on a financial institution's total assets. CET1 (Common Equity Tier 1) : A component of capital that includes common stock and retained earnings, used to measure a bank's financial strength. CLO (Collateralized Loan Obligation) : A single security backed by a pool of debt, often corporate loans. CMBS (Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities) : Fixed-income investment products that are backed by mortgages on commercial properties. GenAI : Generative Artificial Intelligence, used here to provide tools for financial advisors to serve clients. NII (Net Interest Income) : The difference between the revenue a bank generates from its assets and the expenses associated with paying its liabilities. RoTCE (Return on Average Tangible Common Equity) : A financial ratio that measures a company's performance by calculating the return on the equity held by common shareholders, excluding intangible assets.
