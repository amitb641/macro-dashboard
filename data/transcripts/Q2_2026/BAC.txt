INDUSTRY GLOSSARY Net Interest Income (NII) : The difference between the interest income earned on assets like loans and the interest expense paid on liabilities like deposits. Fully Taxable-Equivalent (FTE) : A method of presenting interest income that adjusts for the tax-exempt status of certain loans and investments. Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) : A regulatory capital measure that represents a bank's core equity capital compared to its total risk-weighted assets. Debit Valuation Adjustment (DVA) : An accounting adjustment to the value of a bank's own debt based on changes in the bank's own credit risk. Return on Tangible Common Equity (ROTCE) : A financial ratio that measures a company's net income against its average tangible common shareholders' equity. Assets Under Management (AUM) : The total market value of the investments that a person or entity handles on behalf of clients. Erica : The proprietary AI-driven virtual financial assistant used by Bank of America clients. CashPro : The company's digital platform for corporate and commercial banking clients to manage treasury and payments.
