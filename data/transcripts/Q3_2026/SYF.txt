SUMMARY Synchrony Financial ( SYF +0.52% ) reported record third quarter purchase volume and established a full year 2026 earnings outlook at the high end of its initial guidance range. Management reported that spending growth was strongest among Generation Z and millennial customers, supported by the Prism data analytics and underwriting platform. The company stated that strategic initiatives were focused on integrating its financial ecosystem into emerging commerce channels, including agentic purchasing paths and digital wallets. Management also indicated that credit performance remained stable with delinquency rates in line with the prior year and net charge-offs at a seasonal low. CEO Doubles stated, "Synchrony and Venmo collaborated on a refreshed value proposition for the Venmo credit card... that rewards social spending offering cardholders more cash back for splitting purchases with friends on Venmo." The company announced a new integration with Stripe to embed CareCredit financing into online checkout experiences for more than 12 million existing cardholders. CEO Doubles noted the company is deploying GPT-4 and Astra models "to enable deeper engagement with advanced capabilities, more meaningful product development faster technology innovation." Management reported joining the Personal Agent Protocol, Poppy initiative, as a design partner to help define open standards for artificial intelligence agents in digital commerce. The company introduced CareCredit Pay Later, an installment loan product available at nearly 20,000 health and wellness locations including dental and vision care providers. CFO Wenzel noted that approximately 90% of the 60 basis point year-over-year increase in payment rates is driven by shifts in portfolio and product mix. INDUSTRY GLOSSARY RSA : Retailer Share Arrangement, a contractual agreement where Synchrony shares a portion of program profits with its retail partners. CET1 : Common Equity Tier 1 ratio, a key measure of a bank's capital strength and ability to withstand financial stress. Agentic Commerce : A form of digital commerce where autonomous artificial intelligence agents conduct product discovery and purchases on behalf of consumers. Prism : Synchrony's proprietary data analytics and underwriting platform that uses over 9,000 variables for credit decisioning. Poppy : The Personal Agent Protocol initiative led by Sierra and Meta to develop open standards for AI agents. Dual Card : A credit card that functions as both a private label card for a specific retailer and a general-purpose card on a major network like Visa or Mastercard.
